# Surface brief: home (persuade + experience)

Scope: cezarpretto.dev home and project pages. Audience: companies/founders with a project on paper, plus peers (recruiters, tech leads, hosts). Action: talk to Cezar. Proof: six real cases, real experience and stack. No invented testimonials or metrics. Brand pinned by manual v1.0 (palette, Plex Sans + JetBrains Mono, official logo files). Dark default, light toggle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page performs the brand line "do papel ao produto": a sheet of paper (a brief typed in mono by a cursor) that resolves, as you scroll, into a running product. Refuses the category default of name + role + gradient hero and a grid of same-size cards.
OWN-WORLD: Tinta ground, Papel sheet, Lavanda/Violeta as the only accent, Violeta drenched field for the close. Fixed mono grid rhythm, hairline ruled rows (no card grids), tiny-radius chips, the cursor block as the only recurring ornament. Recognizable with all content removed by: paper sheet beside a dark product panel, ruled rows that invert on hover.
STORY: Visitor understands "software sob medida, do papel ao produto" in one viewport, sees proof in ruled case rows, meets the person via experience with a cursor marking "agora", and closes on a violet field asking for the project.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: H1 "Do papel ao produto." (Plex 600, ~6rem cap) with blinking cursor, one-sentence offer, two CTAs (primary "Falar sobre o seu projeto"), mono meta line with Gupy role. Right: Papel proposal sheet typing its rows, half-overlapped by a dark product panel that fills in as scroll progresses (sticky scene ~240vh).
FORM: Scroll-driven transformation scene (candidate 3 of 7, assigned by roll), fused with ASCII fixed-grid discipline and fixed-column case rows (hover invert; no flap animation). Seed b6e1b9e1.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
